## 💡 My Analytical Approach

I don't start with a dashboard.

I start with the **business question**.

For example:

> **A KPI dropped 12%. Why?**

Instead of immediately creating a visualization, I break the problem down:

```text
Metric Decline
      ↓
When did it begin?
      ↓
Which segments changed?
      ↓
Where is the largest contribution?
      ↓
Is it volume, mix, or rate?
      ↓
What operational factor changed?
      ↓
Can the finding be validated?
      ↓
Business recommendation
```

This approach is the foundation of many of my analytics projects.
